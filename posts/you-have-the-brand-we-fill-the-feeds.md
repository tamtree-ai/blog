Ask a small business owner whether they have a brand strategy and most will say
yes. They know who they sell to, how they sound, what they want to be known for.
Many have it written down. Plenty have paid an agency for it.

Then ask when they last posted a short video. The answer is usually a shrug and
a month.

The strategy was never the hard part. The hard part is the week after it: cutting
a forty-minute webinar into twenty clips, reframing each one for a phone,
captioning it, getting it on-brand, posting it to three platforms at the right
time, and doing all of that again next week. It is not difficult work. It is
just relentless, and it is the first thing to slip when the business gets busy.

## The part that doesn't get done

We hear the same shape of story again and again:

- A physio group records a monthly talk for patients. It goes on YouTube, gets
  forty views, and that is the end of it.
- A consultant has two years of podcast episodes. Not one of them has ever been
  cut into a short.
- A retailer films great clips on a phone at every event and has nobody to edit
  them.

None of these businesses is short of ideas or content. They are short of
*hours*, and the usual fixes all cost more than they are worth. A tool still
needs someone to drive it. An agency often brings its own strategy when you
already have one. Hiring a content person is a salary, and they leave.

## What we built instead

We have been publishing to YouTube, Facebook and Instagram every day on Tamtree,
the workflow platform we build. The same workflow is what we now run for other
businesses. It has four steps, and only one of them is yours:

1. **You send what you have.** Long videos or raw footage go into a shared
   folder, or we watch your YouTube channel. New uploads are picked up on their
   own.
2. **We turn it into shorts.** Best moments are picked against your brand brief,
   reframed vertical, captioned and framed in your fonts and colours. A person
   reviews every batch.
3. **You approve.** Each week, a batch lands in your approval queue. Approve it,
   or send one back with a note.
4. **We publish and report.** Approved shorts go out on schedule, and once a
   month you see what was posted, what was watched and what we will change.

:::note
TikTok and LinkedIn are in development. They join every option at no extra cost
when they go live.
:::

## Why approval is a step, not an email

The most common worry we hear is a fair one: *what if it posts something
stupid?*

In most setups, the answer is a promise. Someone says they will check with you
first, and mostly they do. In Tamtree, approval is a step in the workflow
itself. A short that has not been approved cannot reach the publish step, in the
same way a run cannot skip a step it depends on. Nobody has to remember to ask.
The workflow simply waits, for an hour or a week, until you answer.

The same thinking runs through the rest of the service:

- **Your accounts stay yours.** You connect each one through the platform's own
  sign-in. We never ask for a password, and you can disconnect us at any time.
- **AI is labelled, not hidden.** Where a short uses AI-generated footage or
  voice, the platform's own AI label goes on. No fake reviews, no invented
  customers.
- **Every short is on the record.** Its source, who approved it and when, and
  where it went live. If you ever ask "who posted that?", there is an answer.

## Three options, by what you already have

- **Publish**: you have finished long videos and a brand kit. We turn them into

- **Produce**: you have footage but nothing finished. We add creative direction,
  editing and captions or voiceover, for 12 produced shorts a month.
- **Brand & Launch**: you are starting from scratch. We build the brand kit and
  a 90-day strategy, then run Produce on it.
